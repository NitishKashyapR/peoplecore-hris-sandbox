## Overview

**PeopleCore** is an interactive enterprise Human Resource Information System (HRIS) sandbox and workforce operations academy. It bridges the gap between workforce management theory and practical enterprise operations by providing a realistic, hands-on simulation environment for HR practitioners, people-ops specialists, and workforce analysts.

Rather than reading abstract guidelines or risking changes on live production HR databases, users can explore, configure, and operate end-to-end people operations workflows directly in an interactive platform.

---

## Why PeopleCore? (The Value It Delivers)

Managing modern enterprise workforces requires navigating complex legal regulations, financial calculations, and organizational structures. Mistakes in production environments lead to statutory penalties, compliance breaches, or employee friction. 

PeopleCore solves this challenge by delivering five core values:

### 1. Risk-Free Operational Simulation
* Experience the complete employee lifecycle—from requisition and hiring through onboarding, promotion, leave management, and statutory severance.
* Test operational edge cases (e.g., retro-pay adjustments, disciplinary actions, structural reorganizations) in a safe, simulated sandbox.

### 2. Multi-Jurisdiction Statutory Compliance
* Master global payroll and labor compliance across four major regulatory regimes:
  * **United States**: At-will employment, FLSA overtime rules, FICA/Medicare, Federal and State withholding.
  * **United Kingdom**: Statutory notice periods, PAYE income tax, National Insurance, and UK GDPR data retention rules.
  * **Germany / European Union**: Works council consultation standards, statutory notice caps, Lohnsteuer, and social contribution decomposition.
  * **India**: Payment of Gratuity Act formulas, Provident Fund (PF), and Professional Tax calculations.

### 3. Interactive Formula & Regulatory Laboratories
* Explore the real mathematics driving modern people-operations decisions:
  * **Bradford Factor Lab**: Quantify operational disruption from unplanned absences ($B = S^2 \times D$).
  * **Adverse Impact Science**: Calculate EEOC 4/5ths selection ratios to identify hiring biases.
  * **Global Employment & EOR Risk**: Evaluate co-employment exposure and permanent establishment tax risks when hiring internationally.
  * **Compensation Bands & Compa-Ratio**: Model market benchmarks and salary distribution across grade levels.

### 4. Structured Practical Curriculum & Audio Lectures
* A 10-module workforce curriculum covering foundational data architecture, talent acquisition pipelines, organizational design, performance calibrations, and regulatory audit governance.
* Full-length narrative audio tracks for each module, enabling multi-format learning and conceptual reinforcement.

### 5. Verified Competency Assessment
* Test practical understanding through a 100-question comprehensive examination suite (10 questions per domain, 80% passing threshold).
* Earn a verifiable credential confirming practical competency across all 10 core disciplines.

## Platform Features

  <img src="assets/screenshots/01-command-center-light.png" alt="PeopleCore Command Center" width="100%" />

* **Command Center**: Real-time workforce telemetry, headcount velocity, flight risk indices, and payroll progress tracking.
* **Employee Directory**: Filterable directory with department sorting, status tracking, compensation bands, and direct reporting lines.
* **Lifecycle Onboarding**: Complete wizard for onboarding new hires with jurisdiction assignments, manager hierarchy, and compensation packaging.
* **Bulk Ingestion Pipeline**: Industrial CSV upload engine with column mapping and schema verification.
* **Visual Org Hierarchy**: Dynamic organization tree visualization showing management chains and structural spans.
* **Statutory Payroll Engine**: Live gross-to-net tax decomposition for US, UK, DE, and IN regimes.
* **Talent Acquisition (ATS)**: Multi-stage hiring pipeline with candidate scorecards and stage gating.
* **Compliance & Audit Ledger**: Append-only event history logging all administrative actions with actor attribution.

## Getting Started

PeopleCore requires **zero installation, zero dependencies, and no backend server**. It runs immediately in any modern web browser:

1. **Download**: Save [`peoplecore-hris-sandbox.html`](peoplecore-hris-sandbox.html) to your computer.
2. **Open**: Double-click the downloaded file (or open it with Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge).
3. **Start Practicing**: Explore real HR operations, run payroll simulations, test hiring workflows, and complete the 10 learning modules right away.

You can also view the full product showcase and curriculum overview in [`index.html`](index.html).
